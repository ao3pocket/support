# Privacy Policy — AO3 Pocket

_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

AO3 Pocket is an unofficial, independent iOS client for [Archive of Our Own](https://archiveofourown.org) (AO3). This policy explains what data the app touches and how.

## Data we collect

**None.** AO3 Pocket has no backend server of its own, and its developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

## Your AO3 account

If you sign in, your AO3 username and password are sent directly to Archive of Our Own (archiveofourown.org) over its own standard web session — the same way the AO3 website itself works — and never pass through, or are seen by, any server operated by this app's developer. Your password is stored securely on your device in the iOS Keychain, only so the app can sign you back in automatically; it never leaves your device except to AO3 itself. Your use of AO3 is subject to AO3's own privacy policy and Terms of Service.

## What's stored on your device

Bookmarks, downloaded works, reading progress, highlights, and notes are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). None of this is uploaded to, or accessible by, the developer.

## In-app purchase

AO3 Pocket offers a small, optional, one-time tip to support development. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit) — the developer never sees your payment information. See Apple's own privacy policy for how Apple handles payment data.

## Analytics, tracking, and third parties

AO3 Pocket contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing about your usage is tracked or shared with anyone.

## Permissions

AO3 Pocket does not request access to your camera, photos, location, contacts, or microphone.

## Children's privacy

AO3 Pocket is a client for Archive of Our Own, which hosts user-generated content including works intended for mature/adult audiences, and is not directed at children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: ao3pocket.app@gmail.com

# 隐私政策 — AO3 Pocket

_最后更新:2026 年 9 月 6 日_

AO3 Pocket 是一个非官方的、独立开发的 [Archive of Our Own](https://archiveofourown.org)(AO3)iOS 客户端。以下说明本应用会接触到哪些数据、如何处理。

## 我们收集的数据

**不收集任何数据。** AO3 Pocket 没有自己的后端服务器,开发者不会收集、接收或存储你的任何数据。

## 你的 AO3 账号

如果你登录,你的 AO3 用户名和密码会直接发送给 Archive of Our Own(archiveofourown.org)本身,通过它标准的网页会话完成——跟你直接在 AO3 网站上登录是同一套机制——不会经过开发者运营的任何服务器,开发者也看不到。密码会安全存储在你设备本地的 iOS 钥匙串(Keychain)里,仅用于帮你自动重新登录,除了发给 AO3 本身外不会离开你的设备。你使用 AO3 的行为受 AO3 自己的隐私政策和服务条款约束。

## 存储在你设备上的内容

书签、下载的作品、阅读进度、高亮和笔记都通过苹果的本地存储技术(SwiftData)保存在你设备本地,不会上传给开发者,开发者也无法访问。

## App 内购买

AO3 Pocket 提供一个小额、可选、一次性的打赏,用于支持开发。购买完全由苹果 App Store(StoreKit)处理,开发者看不到你的支付信息。苹果如何处理支付数据请参见苹果自己的隐私政策。

## 分析、追踪与第三方

AO3 Pocket 不含任何分析统计、广告、崩溃报告或第三方 SDK。你的使用行为不会被追踪或分享给任何人。

## 权限

AO3 Pocket 不会请求访问你的相机、照片、位置、通讯录或麦克风。

## 儿童隐私

AO3 Pocket 是 Archive of Our Own 的客户端,该网站包含面向成人的用户生成内容,本应用不面向儿童。

## 联系方式

对本政策有疑问:ao3pocket.app@gmail.com
